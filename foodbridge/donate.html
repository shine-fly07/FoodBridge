Safety Guidelines

Food Safety Tips for Donors

Keep cooked hot meals above 60°C (140°F) or rapidly chill below 5°C (41°F) within 2 hours of preparation. Never leave perishable cooked food at room temperature for over 2 hours.
Use clean, food-grade containers or foil wrapping. Clearly write the preparation time and any major allergens (nuts, dairy, gluten) on the package cover.
Set safe-to-consume windows accurately. If food was prepared 3 hours ago, set the expiry window within the next 2-3 hours so couriers can complete delivery before expiry.
